Fumigation safety policy

Safety first, every spray

We protect your premises, our technicians and your people with a strict safety protocol on every fumigation job.

Book a fumigation
  • Full PPE: neoprene/PVC gloves, overalls, boots & masks
  • Equipment washed immediately after use
  • No eating, drinking or smoking while handling
  • Hands & skin washed with soap before breaks
  • Premises re-occupied within 8 hours of treatment
  • Foodstuffs never exposed to spray
