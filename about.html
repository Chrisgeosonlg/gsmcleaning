Built on integrity, trusted for cleanliness

GSM Cleaning Services is a Tanzanian company providing professional, world-class, modern cleaning to offices and homes — plus fumigation and gardening.

Who we are

A reputation built over the years

We were established to provide professional, world-class, modern cleaning services to offices and residences. Staying dynamic and relevant, we also deliver fumigation and gardening.

In everything we do, we aim to uphold our reputation for acting responsibly, with integrity, while respecting the laws, regulations, traditions and cultures of the nation in which we operate. Our board holds the highest possible standard of professionalism and ethical conduct — codes we maintain to protect our customer and corporate relationships.

Our people

Housekeepers & gardeners you can trust

We recruit Vocational Education Training Authority (VETA) graduates and subject them to a thorough vetting process and intensive interviews. Every team member also completes an internship before formal recruitment — so the people in your space are skilled, screened and dependable.
